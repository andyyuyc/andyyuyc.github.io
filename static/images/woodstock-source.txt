woodstock.png

Woodstock artwork from the official Japanese Snoopy website:
https://www.snoopy.co.jp/characters/woodstock/
Original image (saved without modification):
https://www.snoopy.co.jp/pntswp/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/img_woodstock.png

The birthday animation reuses this PNG in SVG body/wing clips and rotates the
wing with CSS. A short connecting outline closes the body behind the moving
wing. This website animation is custom, not an original Peanuts animation.

Flight motion reference, viewed from the official Peanuts GIPHY account:
https://giphy.com/gifs/peanuts-youre-not-elected-charlie-brown-Ob4TyL7tZVk08
The short climb, loss of balance, tumble and drop inspired the flight timing.
The midair recovery and randomized paths are adaptations for this website.
