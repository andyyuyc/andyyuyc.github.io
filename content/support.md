## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which devices are supported?**
A: Our apps support iOS 16.0 and later on iPhone and iPad.

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Open the App Store, go to your profile, and check for available updates. You can also enable automatic updates in your device settings.

**Q: The app is crashing or not working properly. What should I do?**
A: Please try the following steps:
1. Make sure you have the latest version of the app installed.
2. Restart the app by closing it completely and reopening it.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the issue persists, contact us at [support@ayxr.net](mailto:support@ayxr.net) with a description of the problem and your device model/iOS version.

**Q: How do I request a new feature?**
A: We'd love to hear your ideas! Send us an email at [support@ayxr.net](mailto:support@ayxr.net) with your suggestion.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. We take your privacy seriously. Please see our Privacy Policy below for details.

## Privacy Policy

AYXR respects your privacy. Our apps:

- Do **not** collect personal information unless explicitly provided by you.
- Do **not** share your data with third parties.
- Do **not** use tracking or analytics that identify individual users.

If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us at [support@ayxr.net](mailto:support@ayxr.net).

## Bug Reports & Feedback

To report a bug or share feedback, please email us at [support@ayxr.net](mailto:support@ayxr.net) and include:

- **App name and version**
- **Device model** (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Pro)
- **iOS version**
- **A description of the issue** or your feedback

We appreciate your help in making our apps better!
