Yichen Andy Yu

I am a first-year PhD student at North Carolina State University, working with Prof. Qiao Jin, and collaborating with Prof. Lei Zhang and Prof. April Wang. My research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction, particularly AR/VR Interactions, CS Education, and Accessibility. Previously, I completed my Master degree at the University of Rochester, where I worked with Prof. Zhen Bai and Prof. Dillon Dzikowicz, and have published research at CHI, UIST, Ubicomp, ISMAR and other venues.
